Thank you for reviewing Tsuioku LiveView.

WHAT IT IS
A companion app for the Chrome extension "君斗りんくの追憶のきらめき" (Chrome Web Store, published). The extension runs on the user's own PC while they watch a Niconico Live stream, and this app shows the same supporter view (comments, gifts, supporter ranking) on iPhone. No account or login is needed. No in-app purchase, no ads.

HOW TO TEST WITHOUT A PC
A. Live supporter ranking (real public data, no PC needed)
1. Launch the app. The start screen opens.
2. Tap "🏆 いま配信中の応援ランキング" (Live supporter ranking). It lists Niconico Live streams that are on air now, with the supporters Niconico itself publishes (gift and ad rankings). It refreshes every 60 seconds.

B. Demo stream
1. On the start screen, tap "デモを見る" (View demo). A demo stream with made-up viewers and comments plays on the device. New comments arrive every few seconds and a gift arrives about every 20 seconds.

NATIVE FEATURES (only in the app, not on the website)
- Gift notifications + haptics: in the demo, tap the 🔕 button on the right side. iOS asks for notification permission. While the app is open, each new gift gives a haptic tap. While the screen is locked or the app is in the background and read-aloud is on, each new gift shows a local notification.
- Background read-aloud (UIBackgroundModes: audio): in the demo, tap 🗣 to start reading new comments aloud with the iPhone's built-in voice (AVSpeechSynthesizer). Lock the screen: the read-aloud continues.
- Share sheet: tap 📤 to share the stream page with the native share sheet.

USER-GENERATED CONTENT
Comments shown are public comments from Niconico Live streams the user is watching on their own PC. The app does not host or collect comments on a server.
- Hide a user: tap 🚩 and tap "隠す" (Hide) next to a recent commenter. Their comments and avatar disappear from this device. "戻す" (Undo) restores them.
- Report: the same 🚩 screen has "ニコニコに報告する" (report to Niconico, the service that hosts the comments) and "このアプリに連絡する" (email us).
